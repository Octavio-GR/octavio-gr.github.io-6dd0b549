# EVIDENCE

Toroide distinguishes between different kinds of evidence.

Nothing should become a permanent claim unless at least one form of evidence exists.

---

## Claim

A statement.

Example:

"This system reduces uncertainty."

---

## Observation

Something a visitor can directly perceive.

Examples:

- Navigation hierarchy
- Loading behavior
- Typography consistency

---

## Artifact

Something inspectable.

Examples:

- Source code
- Documentation
- ADR
- Audit
- Exhibit

---

## Measurement

Something reproducible.

Examples:

- Performance metrics
- Accessibility measurements
- Bundle size
- Validation results
